Where provenance is recorded
----------------------------

Assets carry KiCad's own fields only. Licence, copyright and source are recorded
once, in ASSET_PROVENANCE.csv, rather than duplicated into every asset.

STEP files additionally carry licence, copyright and source in their own
ISO 10303-21 FILE_DESCRIPTION and FILE_NAME header, and the FreeCAD projects
carry them in their document properties. Those are the file formats' own
metadata, not added fields, and there are no sidecar files.


Manufacturer datasheet links
----------------------------

Datasheet and Source entries identify official manufacturer webpages used as
factual references for dimensions and pin functions. A hyperlink does not copy,
redistribute, or relicense the linked document.

Dimensional facts taken from those documents - lead spans, pad layouts, body
outlines, pin functions - are not themselves copyrightable subject matter. The
CAD expression of them in this repository is original work.

No manufacturer PDF is held in this repository.
generate_original_kicad_assets.py emits a datasheet provenance row only when a
PDF is present in the repository root, and marks any such row
not_cleared_for_redistribution.
